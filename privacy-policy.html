CatLove Privacy Policy

Last updated: [18 September 2026]

CatLove is a cat-care game for iPhone and iPad made by [Sam]. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your cat's name and breed, its stats, your coins, and the items you have bought are saved only on your device. We do not run a server, we do not ask you to create an account, and we never receive this information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your cat gets hungry). They are created and delivered locally; nothing is sent to us.

Advertising

CatLove shows ads through Google AdMob. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch one to earn a reward. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance.

If you do not allow tracking, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children

CatLove is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
